Good to Know

Frequently asked questions.

Do you provide the alcohol?

No — you supply the alcohol for your event. We bring the ingredients and garnish for your three signature cocktails, plus 12oz plastic cups, ice, and full bar setup.

What's included in every booking?

Full bar setup and clean-up, plus ingredients and garnish for three signature cocktails, 12oz plastic cups, and ice. You'll need to provide the alcohol and glassware. Add-ons — an extra signature cocktail ($25), an extra hour ($60), and custom event signage (from $20) — are available on every package.

How far in advance should we book?

2–4 weeks for weeknight or smaller events, 6+ weeks for weekend celebrations and peak season (May–September, plus the holidays).

Can we do a mocktail-only event?

Yes — full zero-proof menus are a regular part of what we do, built with the same care and garnish as a spirited bar.

What's your service area?

Within 30 miles of Colorado Springs, CO. Travel fees may apply beyond that.
